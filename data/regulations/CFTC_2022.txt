CFTC (2022) – Commodity Futures Reporting FAQ
-------------------------------------------------
Q1: What is the official name of the reporting system?
A1: The system is called the Commodity Futures Trading Commission Trade Reporting System (CFTC‑TRS).

Q2: Which trades must be reported?
A2: All futures, options on futures, swaps and other derivatives that are executed on U.S. exchanges or clearing houses must be reported. The report must include Trade_ID, Asset_Class, Execution_Timestamp, Counterparty_LEI (if available), Price, Quantity, Currency, and Notional_Amount.

Q3: Are negative price values allowed?
A3: No – the price field must be a non‑negative numeric value. Submissions that contain a negative price will be rejected and returned with error code E‑001.

Q4: What is the deadline for reporting a trade?
A4: Trades must be reported within 15 minutes of execution for standardised contracts and within one business day for non‑standardised contracts.

Q5: Do we need to include a LEI for every counterparty?
A5: A valid 20‑character LEI is required for any counterparty that is a registered entity with a GLEIF‑accredited Local Operating Unit. If the counterparty is not LEI‑registered, the field may be left blank, but the firm must retain an internal identifier for audit purposes.

Q6: How long must we retain the reports and supporting documentation?
A6: All reports and supporting documentation (trade confirmations, broker statements, etc.) must be retained for a minimum of seven (7) years in a format that allows electronic retrieval.

Q7: What are the penalties for non‑compliance?
A7: The CFTC may impose civil monetary penalties up to USD 5 million per violation, and in cases of willful non‑compliance, criminal penalties may also be pursued.

Q8: Is there a list of approved currency codes?
A8: Yes – the CFTC accepts the ISO 4217 three‑letter currency codes. The most common are USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, and CHF. Use of any other code must be justified and documented.

Q9: Where can we find the technical specifications for the trade report file format?
A9: The technical specifications are published on the CFTC website under “Reporting Guidelines – File Specification v2.1”. They include the exact field order, data types and length restrictions.

Q10: How are amendments to previously submitted trades handled?
A10: Amendments must be submitted as a separate file with the same Trade_ID and a flag “Amendment=Y”. The amendment file must include the corrected fields and a timestamp indicating the amendment date.
